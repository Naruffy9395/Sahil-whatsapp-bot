Seedhi baat

Business ke liye alag number ya SIM use karein, apna personal WhatsApp na jodein. Bot sirf us number ki WhatsApp chats dekhta hai. SMS, gallery aur baaki apps tak uski pahunch nahi.

Ek risk jo hum chhupate nahi: ye WhatsApp ka official tareeka nahi, WhatsApp Web jaisa link hai. Isliye bahut kam sambhavna hai ki WhatsApp us number par rok laga de. Bot sirf unhe jawab deta hai jo pehle message karein.

Aap kabhi bhi WhatsApp mein Settings, phir Linked Devices se bot ko hata sakte hain.
