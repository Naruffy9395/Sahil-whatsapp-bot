Shuru kaise hota hai

  1. Neeche naam, number aur business batayein. Aapki request seedhi mere WhatsApp par aayegi.
  2. Main aapke business ke 5 chhote sawal puchhunga: kya bechte hain, rate, timing, pata.
  3. Bot ko aapke number se jod diya jata hai (5 minute ka kaam).
  4. Pehle chhota trial. Pasand na aaye to kuch nahi dena.

Seedhi baat

Aapka personal data safe rahe isliye business ke liye alag number ya SIM use karein. Bot sirf us number ki WhatsApp chats dekhta hai. SMS, gallery aur baaki apps tak uski pahunch nahi.

Ek risk jo hum chhupate nahi: ye WhatsApp ka official tareeka nahi, WhatsApp Web jaisa link hai. Isliye bahut kam sambhavna hai ki WhatsApp us number par rok laga de. Bot sirf unhe jawab deta hai jo pehle message karein.

Aap kabhi bhi WhatsApp mein Settings, phir Linked Devices se bot ko hata sakte hain.
